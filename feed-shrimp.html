अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल (FAQ)

Shrimp feed kaise banaye? (झींगा का feed घर पर कैसे बनाएं?)

Shrimp feed banane ke liye kachcha maal (Soybean meal / DOC, Mustard cake (Sarso khali), Rice bran / DORB, Corn / Maize, Wheat bran / Rice polish, Fish meal / Meat-chicken meal) ko peeskar barik powder banayein, achhi tarah milayein, phir Vela Peptide additives (amino acids, enzymes, probiotics, vitamins aur minerals) milakar pellet machine se pellet ya goliyan bana lein aur chhaanv mein sukha lein.

Shrimp feed mein kaun sa kachcha maal lagta hai?

Shrimp feed ke liye mukhya kachcha maal: Soybean meal / DOC, Mustard cake (Sarso khali), Rice bran / DORB, Corn / Maize, Wheat bran / Rice polish, Fish meal / Meat-chicken meal. Protein ke liye soybean meal, sarso khali aur fish meal; urja ke liye makka, rice bran aur gehun chokar.

Shrimp ki growth tez kaise karein?

Santulit protein wala feed, saaf paani, sahi sankhya mein machhli (stocking) aur roz samay par feeding zaruri hai. Feed mein amino acids, enzymes aur probiotics milane se Shrimp feed ka behtar upyog karti hai aur growth sudharti hai.

Shrimp feed mein additives kitne pratishat daalein?
